Gen Mix.

Advancing Intergenerational Connection and Digital Equity to Combat Senior Isolation

Gen Mix is a proposed six-week program that pairs older adults with high school volunteers. Together, participants build confidence with everyday technology and make space for conversation, shared experience, and connection.

What to expect

  1. 01

    Digital literacy, first

    One-on-one help with useful skills such as using a device, joining a video call, and navigating everyday digital tools. Sessions can adapt to what each participant wants to learn.

  2. 02

    Conversation and connection

    Recurring one-on-one conversations and randomly selected prompts create opportunities to connect, exchange perspectives, and share stories at a comfortable pace.

  3. 03

    Before-and-after activities

    Participants may be invited to complete brief memory and recall activities before and after the program, alongside check-ins about loneliness and technology confidence. These are exploratory research activities, not a diagnosis or medical assessment.
